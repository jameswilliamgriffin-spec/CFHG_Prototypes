Membership and meetings

Your first visit is free. After that it is £5 a visit, or £20 for a year’s membership.

When and where we meet

The group meets on the last Saturday of the month, except May, August and December. Meetings are in the computer room on the first floor of Solihull Core Library and on Zoom, so you can join from anywhere.

What happens at a meeting

Each meeting has an agenda, often a guest speaker or a member’s talk, and plenty of time to work on your own research with help from others. Bring a laptop if you have one, and whatever you already know about your family.

Joining

  • First visit: free.
  • After that: £5 a visit.
  • Annual membership: £20. Payments are taken through the Family History Federation.

Membership is open to anyone aged 18 or over, and younger people are welcome with a parent or guardian.

Photographs and video

We take photographs and video at some events. If you would rather not appear, fill in our non-permission form and send it to the group, and we will make sure you are left out.

The committee

The group is run by a core committee of volunteers elected at the AGM each May. Roles include Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, Membership Secretary, Events Co-ordinator, Newsletter Co-ordinator, Website Administrator, Senior Researcher and Military Research Expert. Anyone who has been active in the group for six months can stand. We all give our time freely, so expectations are flexible.
