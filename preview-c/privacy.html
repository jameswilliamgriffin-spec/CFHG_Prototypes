Privacy and cookies

How we look after the information you share with us.

The Caribbean Family History Group is committed to protecting your privacy and making sure your personal data is handled safely and responsibly. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights when you use our website. Last updated 10 October 2025.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, for example through contact forms or event enquiries.
  • The content of messages you send us.
  • Website usage data collected through cookies, such as pages viewed.
  • Device and browser information, such as IP address and general location.

We do not collect sensitive personal data through the website.

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries about genealogy, events or getting involved.
  • To send updates if you have subscribed.
  • To improve the website and keep it secure.
  • To keep basic records of enquiries and community engagement.

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 12 months unless further contact continues. Analytics data is kept for the standard period set by our analytics provider.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw consent, object to processing, and ask for your data in a portable form. Email cfhg.sol.birm@gmail.com to do any of these.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep the site working and cannot be switched off. Analytics cookies help us understand how the site is used and only run if you accept them. Functionality cookies remember choices such as your cookie preference. Your text size choice is stored in your own browser and is not sent to us.
