Researching the British West India Regiment

Two regiments with similar names, two sets of records. Where to look for a soldier in the family.

Many Caribbean families have a soldier in their story. Before you search, check which regiment you are looking for, because two have very similar names.

  • The West India Regiments were raised in 1795 and served until 1927.
  • The British West Indies Regiment was raised in 1915 for the First World War, and served in Egypt, Palestine, Italy, France and Belgium until 1919.

Where the records are

The National Archives in Kew holds most of the surviving service records. Papers for soldiers discharged before 1913 are in series WO 97. Many First World War service records were destroyed by bombing in 1940; those that survive are in WO 363 and WO 364. Medal index cards in WO 372 cover most men who served overseas.

Our military research lead can help with questions at any meeting.
