Who we are

We love family history, and learning about and claiming our Caribbean heritage. We exist to share that, and to help you do the same.

Helping people research since 2008

The group began in 2008, when courses in Caribbean family history were made from a ten-week British family history beginners’ class and reshaped for people with Caribbean heritage.

Some of our founder members are still on the committee today. Newer members have joined them, so between us we have a wide range of experience in researching ancestors across the Caribbean.

Solihull Library

We helped Solihull Library become an Associate Family History Centre. Inside the library you can use almost all of FamilySearch’s online Caribbean records. At home, what you can see depends on the island: Jamaica and Barbados are almost fully open.

If you would like help with your search, tell us.

What we do

We meet on the last Saturday of the month, at Solihull Core Library and on Zoom for anyone who can’t be there. We also go to family history fairs and cultural events around the country, and put on exhibitions such as our Windrush and Black History Month displays.

Who we work with

  • Solihull Libraries

    Our meetings happen at Solihull Core Library. At our request it became a FamilySearch affiliate in 2011, and every Solihull library now gives free access to FamilySearch’s digitised records.

  • The WAWI Project

    Why Are West Indians in this Country. We have worked alongside each other since around 2010 and host the Windrush Dinner and Dance together every June.

  • Family History Federation

    We are a member society of the Federation, which promotes family history research and handles our membership payments.
