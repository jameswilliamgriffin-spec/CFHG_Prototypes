01 — Why now

A site that's done its job — built for what's next

The current site has done exactly what it needed to: it has kept members connected, published news and events, and given CFHG a home online for years. But the organisation — and the audience it is trying to reach — has moved on. A new generation of Caribbean family historians expects something fresh, interactive, and easy to use on a phone, not just a desktop.

11 — What is Sanity?

A content engine built for this site

Sanity is a headless CMS — a content backend that stores everything editors need to update (news, events, photos, sub-group dates, homepage highlights) and feeds it straight into the website's design, with no code involved.

Fully bespokeBuilt around the actual sitemap, not a generic template.
Focused editingEditors see only the sections they need.
Fast by designStructured content paired natively with Next.js.
Smaller security surfaceNo plugins to maintain or patch.
Free tier covers itComfortably covers the project’s needs.
More custom, fasterPage types without fighting a page-builder.

12 — What is WordPress?

An all-in-one website platform

WordPress combines the website itself with its own built-in content editor, working through themes (ready-made designs) and plugins (add-on features) rather than needing custom development for every piece.

Extremely well-knownA future volunteer or developer is very likely to already be familiar with it.
Huge plugin ecosystemContact forms, SEO tools and event calendars, added without custom development.
Mature & well-documentedDecades of tutorials and community support to draw on.
Wide hosting choiceProviders like Fasthosts offer WordPress hosting at every price point.
