Who we are

Volunteers who enjoy genealogy, helping each other and anyone who asks to trace families with roots in the Caribbean.

We are like-minded people who enjoy family history research. We are all still learning, and we share our resources and what we have found along the way.

Some of us have been researching for more than forty years; some of us started a few months ago. A few of us are experts in particular islands or in military records. Since the pandemic moved our meetings online, members have joined us from across the UK, the USA, Jamaica and Trinidad.

Our history

In 2006 and 2007, Solihull Libraries and Solihull College ran a series of ten-week family history courses for people tracing ancestors from the Caribbean. They were the first full-length Caribbean family history courses in the UK, and were funded by the European Social Fund’s Equal Engage programme.

At our request, Solihull Central Library became an affiliate FamilySearch Centre in 2011, so that members could build up a collection of Caribbean microfilms at the library for anyone to use. Until then, most of those records were not online, and members travelled to the London Family History Centre to see them.

In 2017 the microfilm service was replaced by online access to digitised collections, which are free to use at FamilySearch affiliate libraries. Solihull became the first local authority in the UK to have every one of its libraries designated as a FamilySearch affiliate.

Every library in Solihull now gives free access to FamilySearch’s digitised Caribbean records.

Who we work with

Solihull Libraries

Our meetings take place at Solihull Core Library, and each year the library gives us space for a Black History Month exhibition.

The WAWI Project

Why Are West Indians in this Country has been our community partner since around 2010. We work closely together and promote each other’s events, and when WAWI is invited to speak at community events they make time for us to say a few words too. Together we host the Windrush Dinner and Dance every June.

Family History Federation

The Federation promotes family history research and encourages people to join its member societies. We are a member, and our membership payments are handled through the Federation.

Meetings and membership

  • We meet on the last Saturday of the month, except in May, August and December.
  • Meetings are on Zoom and in the computer room on the first floor of Solihull Core Library.
  • Your first visit is free. After that it is £5 a visit, or £20 a year for membership.
  • Our AGM is held every May, when committee roles open up.

Photographs and videos

We take photographs and videos at our events. Coming to an event means you agree to appear in them, unless you have filled in and returned our non-permission form.

Your privacy

We only collect what we need to reply to you and run the group: usually your name, your email address and your message. We do not sell or share your details for marketing, and we keep enquiry emails for up to 12 months unless we are still in touch. You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you at any time.

Contact us

Email cfhg.sol.birm@gmail.com. We are all volunteers, so a reply can take a little while.
